Shared data model · job (JOB-####)

Booking calendar — assets + workforce

The calendar is the join between the three domains: a won SALE (Sales) becomes a JOB that books assets (Assets) and employees (Workforce) over a date range. A resource can't be double-booked on overlapping dates.
